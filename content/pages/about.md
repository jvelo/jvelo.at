I'm a freelance software product engineer and tech lead based in Toulouse, France.

I published my first websites as a teenager in the late 90's. Back then, I preferred fiddling 
around with Notepad and FileZilla to Dreamweaver, even though that meant I didn't understand 
everything I was doing. Before Google came around, I have fond memory of using `View Source` to 
try and copy some style or effect from other people's websites.

I've since made it my job, first as a software developer, then into tech leadership and product 
work.
